How Symphonia Works

Most conflict isn't about what was said — it's about how it was heard. Every person listens through the filter of their own wiring, and Symphonia helps couples, churches, teams, and businesses finally understand those filters so communication becomes clear instead of chaotic.

Symphonia uses a certified temperament framework to assess how people are wired, reveal the real communication patterns driving friction, and equip every group with practical tools they can use long after the session ends. Whether it's a marriage needing a reset, a church staff navigating personality differences, a business strengthening leadership communication, or a team improving chemistry, Symphonia turns "personality clashes" into shared understanding.

Led by Lindsay Peaster, a certified presenter in Kathleen Edelman's I Said This, You Heard That framework — and trusted through years of work in local schools and youth sports — Symphonia brings clarity, connection, and a proven approach from someone your community already knows.
